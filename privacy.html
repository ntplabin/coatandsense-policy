Build a coating layer by layer, choose real materials, and watch reflectance (R), transmittance (T) and absorptance (A) change instantly. Use it to learn how anti-reflection coatings, dielectric mirrors and semi-transparent metal films work, or to make a quick first estimate before a detailed design. DESIGN • Stack up to 30 layers on a substrate; drag to reorder, duplicate or delete layers • Real optical constants (n and k) for common materials: MgF2, SiO2, Al2O3, TiO2, Ta2O5, Si3N4, ZnS, ZnO, CaF2, Si, Ge, Ag, Au, Al, Cu and Cr • Quarter-wave helper: set any layer to a quarter-wave optical thickness at your design wavelength and read thickness in QWOT • One-tap presets: single-layer anti-reflection, quarter-wave mirror, aluminium mirror, semi-transparent silver SIMULATE • Wavelength range 200 to 2200 nm (ultraviolet to near-infrared) • Angle of incidence from 0 to 89 degrees, with s, p or averaged polarisation; the Brewster angle is shown • Optional finite substrate with back-surface reflection and substrate absorption • Choose the incident and exit media EXPLORE • Live R, T and A readout that stays visible while you edit • Plot against wavelength, angle of incidence, or the thickness of any layer • Touch and drag the chart to move the marker • Minimum, average and maximum values, plus visible-band averages • See the colour of the coating as it appears in reflection REPORT AND SHARE • Professional PDF report: configuration, layer table, results, plots and data • Export to Excel or CSV with the full configuration at the top • Save the plot as an image • Saved files open automatically PRIVATE AND SIMPLE • No account or sign-up • Your settings are saved on your device and restored next time; clear them with one tap • Calculations run offline using material data built into the app ABOUT THE DATA AND METHOD Optical constants come from the public refractiveindex.info database. Results are computed with the transfer-matrix method. Beyond a dataset's measured range the nearest value is used, and the app tells you when that happens. Coat and Sense is intended for education and preliminary design; validate critical designs against measurements and professional design software. The app contains ads. Privacy policy: <> Developed by Optical Spark. ``` ### What's new (release notes, max 500) ```text Version 2.0 • New simulation engine: angle of incidence, s/p polarisation, finite substrates, plots vs wavelength, angle and thickness • Real material data from refractiveindex.info • Redesigned interface with live results, presets and a first-run tour • Professional PDF reports; Excel, CSV and plot-image export • Settings saved automatically, one-tap clear • Login and cloud sync removed: everything stays on your device ``` --- ## 2.
