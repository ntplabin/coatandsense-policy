Privacy Policy

Coat and Sense · Last updated: 15 November 2026

In short: Coat and Sense runs on your device. There is no account or login, and we do not operate a server that receives your data. Ads are provided by Google AdMob.

Summary

Coat and Sense is a calculator for thin-film optical coatings. It works on your device. There is no account or login, and we do not run a server that receives your data.

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Those ads are the only part of the app that involves collecting data, and that data is handled by Google, not by us.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves your simulation settings on your device only:

  • the layer stack (materials and thicknesses), substrate and incident medium
  • angle, polarisation, wavelengths and plot settings
  • whether the introductory tour has been shown

This information never leaves your device. Use the Clear button (the restart icon in the top bar) to delete it at any time, or uninstall the app.

Files you create

Reports (PDF, XLSX, CSV) and plot images are generated on your device. They are saved or shared only when you choose to do so, using the system dialogs. We never receive them.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). AdMob may collect and use information such as your advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information and ad interactions, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where it is required, Google's consent message is shown before ads are requested, so you can accept or refuse personalised advertising.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Network use

Apart from ads, the app uses the internet only when you open the Developer page (it loads images from Google servers) or follow a link, which opens your browser or the Play Store. The optical-constant data used for calculations is bundled in the app; nothing is downloaded or uploaded for calculations.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to display ads and the Developer page
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob for ads
  • The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or storage.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

We do not hold personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You can clear the settings stored on your device, change your ad choices as described above, or uninstall the app. For data processed by Google for ads, see Google's policies or contact Google.

Third-party services

Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated and the date above changed.
